# Portfolio and process feedback

This is a formative feedback frame. It supplies no institutional grade, weighted percentages, certification, or validated measure. A facilitator may adapt it to local rules after checking access and assessment requirements.

## Suggested portfolio

- Two or three pieces chosen by the writer, including at least one before/after revision.
- Brief annotations of craft decisions: what changed in imagery, pacing, voice, or form, and why.
- A [Personal Creative AI Protocol](../templates/creative-ai-protocol.md), including an offline route.
- A contribution record for outside suggestions where the setting requires disclosure.
- One open question about the writing or the protocol.

An offline portfolio is complete. No one must submit a breath log, body map, trauma narrative, private journal, or AI transcript.

## Feedback dimensions

| Dimension | Ask |
| --- | --- |
| Craft intention | Can the writer name a choice and its hoped-for effect without claiming certainty about a reader? |
| Revision | Do the before/after versions and note show a reasoned change or a reasoned decision to keep a feature? |
| Evidence and attribution | Are factual assertions checked where needed and outside contributions handled transparently? |
| Agency | Can the writer explain what they accepted, altered, or declined, with or without AI? |
| Reflection | Does the writer identify one limitation or question that remains? |

Respond first to the writer's stated aim, then offer one specific observation and one question. Avoid scoring “deep embodiment,” nervous system regulation, or adherence to a single theory of human–AI relationship. A single portfolio cannot establish long-term creative development or the effectiveness of this course.
