# Embodied Creative Writing

Status: v0.1 draft · Formative educational resources · Not piloted

Embodied Creative Writing is an open learning pathway from Trivian Institute for writers and educators exploring attention, sensory detail, voice, revision, and ethical collaboration with AI. Writers can complete every activity without an AI account. Embodiment is an invitation to notice and make choices, never a test of authenticity, wellness, or artistic worth.

## Begin

| If you want to | Start here | Time |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Run a complete workshop | [Sensory scene and source](sessions/sensory-scene-and-source.md) | 75 min |
| Explore voice without surrendering authorship | [Voice by contrast](exercises/voice-by-contrast.md) | 30–45 min |
| Revise with attention to rhythm and choice | [Revision through attention](exercises/revision-through-attention.md) | 35–50 min |
| Set boundaries for creative collaboration | [Personal Creative AI Protocol](templates/creative-ai-protocol.md) | 30 min |

[Course overview](COURSE_OVERVIEW.md) maps these practices into an adaptable eight-week sequence. [Facilitation choices](facilitation/choice-and-privacy.md) cover access, consent, privacy, and alternatives. [Portfolio and process](assessment/portfolio-and-process.md) offers feedback criteria focused on craft and reasoning rather than bodily state.

## Across the Commons

[Embodiment Commons](https://github.com/TrivianInstitute/embodiment-commons) provides optional attention practices. Trivian Technologies' technical implementations and the Ahava Way certification curriculum are outside its scope.

## Scope and provenance

The pathway is newly authored from the educational concept of somatic writing and ethical AI collaboration. An earlier academic syllabus and the unpublished *Writing the Breath* manuscript informed its direction, but neither is distributed here. No manuscript excerpts, original syllabus, contact details, private student material, or unpublished creative pieces are included. The course map and activities are drafts, not a tested program or a promise of therapeutic benefit.
