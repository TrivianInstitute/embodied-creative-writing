# Contributing

Trivian Institute welcomes educators, writers, and readers who can make these resources clearer and more accessible. Contact learn@trivianinstitute.org.

Open an issue for a non-identifying correction, broken link, access barrier, or teaching suggestion. Propose text changes in a pull request. Say which resource and audience you have in mind, what you changed, what you observed if you have actually taught it, and what remains untested. Do not paste a participant's writing or private experience into an issue or PR. For a public field note, follow [Learning in Practice](https://github.com/TrivianInstitute/learning-in-practice).

Submit only original material you can share under this repository's [CC BY-SA 4.0 license](LICENSE). Contributors retain copyright; no assignment is requested. Do not submit unpublished manuscript excerpts, proprietary syllabi, other people's writing, identifiable student material, private prompts, or outside copyrighted passages without a separate rights review. The unpublished *Writing the Breath* manuscript and the private academic syllabus are outside this repository.

For substantive AI assistance, disclose the provider/system if known, approximate date, tasks assisted, material changes made by you, and checks. Never reveal private prompts or learner data for disclosure. Maintainers may request revision or decline material for rights, evidence, accessibility, or fit. See [community expectations](CODE_OF_CONDUCT.md).
